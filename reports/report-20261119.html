Separating the soaking and deformation temperatures provides a means of tailoring the elevated-temperature response of Ti-6Al-4V sheet before rapid forming. Direct-heating (DH) and step-quenching (SQ) tensile responses were compared at subtransus temperatures and nominal strain rates of 0.1, 1 and 5 s-1. Numerical Investigation of Forming Limits in Zn–Ag–Cu-Based Alloy Sheets Using Estimated Anisotropic Constitutive Properties (2026-11-16)

Atilla Uğurlu
Brilliant Engineering
利用估计的各向异性本构特性研究Zn–Ag–Cu基合金板的成形极限。
In the fabrication of biomedical sheet components from next-generation Zn-Ag-Cu-based alloys, establishing the relationship between the material's directional plastic behavior and the limits of multiaxial forming is of critical importance. This study presents an integrated analytical-numerical approach developed to convert the mechanical properties of solid alloys into direction-dependent sheet properties and forming limit diagrams. A distinctive feature of this methodology is the theoretical estimation of plastic anisotropy coefficients based on a proposed analytical approximation that incorporates alloy composition and crystal lattice geometry. These estimated coefficients were incorporated into a finite element model via directional yield stresses. For three independent datasets, elastic properties were determined using the Voigt-Reuss-Hill approach, hardening behavior was modeled with the Hollomon equation, and the anisotropic yield surface was approximated using the Hill48 criterion. The resulting synthetic sheet material cards were utilized in virtual Nakajima tests involving seven specimen geometries, where the onset of local necking was determined from time-dependent changes in strain histories at critical points. While the minimum predicted major limit strains ranged from 0.038 to 0.111 across the datasets in paths near plane strain, the major limit strain in the uniaxial loading region reached 0.371. In contrast, the limits in the biaxial tensile region converged within the range of 0.328–0.345. This analysis demonstrates that formability differences among material models become particularly evident in deformation paths close to uniaxial and in-plane strain. These findings represent preliminary computational predictions, and further experimental microstructural testing is required to verify actual sheet formability.

Magnesium alloys hold substantial potential for lightweight armor applications due to their low density and high specific strength. However, their low absolute strength has restricted their large–scale engineering applications. To address this problem, explosive hardening (EH) was employed to fabricate a gradient nanostructure (GNS) with a depth of 14 mm in AZ31 magnesium alloy (AZ31 Mg alloy), thereby enhancing its mechanical properties and ballistic performance. Experimental and numerical simulation methods were integrated to investigate the microstructure, mechanical properties, and ballistic performance of the GNS AZ31 Mg alloy. Mechanical tests reveal significant improvements in GNS AZ31 compared to untreated specimens: a 63% increase in microhardness, a 107% enhancement in tensile yield strength, and a 59% rise in dynamic compressive yield strength. Ballistic tests yield an observed 6.3% rise in fitted ballistic limit (372 m/s for GNS AZ31 versus 350 m/s for the untreated alloy). Both targets display petal–shaped cracking under low–velocity perforation and plugging failure at high impact velocities. The untreated AZ31 Mg alloy suffers plugging failure at 368 m/s, whereas GNS AZ31 still shows prominent petal–shaped cracking at 384 m/s, indicating delayed shear deformation during perforation. Finite element (FE) simulations confirm a multi-stage energy-dissipation mechanism in GNS AZ31. Gradient-matched layer properties allow the high-strength surface to withstand initial projectile impact and lower-strength inner layers to dissipate residual energy. This hierarchical gradient design improves energy-absorption capacity, suggesting EH-induced GNS is a promising route toward advanced lightweight armor materials.

A unified strain energy decomposition framework for phase-field fracture modelling (2026-10-28)
相场断裂模型中统一的应变能分解框架
Yang Jiang, Cunyi Li, Chi Wu, Qing Li, Jianguang Fang
International Journal of Mechanical Sciences
对相场断裂模型中的材料，提出统一的应变能分解方法，将弹性应变能分为拉伸、剪切和压缩三部分，实现任意应力状态下的断裂演化模拟。
In the phase-field modelling framework, strain energy is commonly decomposed into positive and negative components to capture the tension–compression asymmetry of materials. Nevertheless, most existing decomposition strategies are valid only for specific stress-state cases, limiting their applicability in simulating fracture propagation in real-world structures that often experience complex and varying stress conditions. To overcome this limitation, a unified strain energy decomposition framework applicable to arbitrary stress states is proposed. Within this framework, the elastic strain energy is divided into three non-negative components, namely tensile, shear and compressive strain energy. A local coordinate system is determined by maximising the F -function energy dissipation, which drives the damage evolution. Depending on the ratio of shear to tensile fracture toughness and the value of Poisson’s ratio, the proposed unified framework can degenerate into existing decomposition schemes such as the spectral, volumetric–deviatoric (V–D), or crack-direction-based decomposition methods under specific stress states. The unified phase-field model, implemented in ABAQUS platform through the VUEL and VUMAT subroutines, was verified via a single-element benchmark and subsequently validated through a series of numerical examples under tensile, compressive, and shear stress states. Those results illustrated that the proposed energy decomposition framework consistently reproduces fracture evolution across arbitrary stress states, showing superior generality, robustness, and accuracy compared with currently existing decomposition approaches.

A constitutive framework for fracture mode diagram of hydrogen-degraded steels: coupled void damage, quasi-cleavage, and rate-dependent transport (2026-11-16)
氢退化钢的断裂模式图的本构框架：耦合空洞损伤、准解理和速率依赖的传输
Rui Wang, Jiaqing Li, Che Zhang, Hui Wang, Cheng Lü
International Journal of Hydrogen Energy
对氢退化钢，建立耦合空洞损伤、准解理和速率依赖传输的本构框架，模拟不同应力状态下的断裂模式。
Hydrogen-charged steels often show quasi-cleavage (QC) near the surface and ductile dimples in the bulk. In the proposed model, void nucleation, growth, distortion, coalescence and QC compete at each material point under a single damage criterion. The QC rate law describes hydrogen-enhanced decohesion mediated by localised plasticity (HELP-mediated HEDE), driven by the Mori–Tanaka-recovered matrix stress and gated by hydrogen availability, and only QC and distortion damage are regularised non-locally. Coupled with hydrogen transport, the model is applied to X70-type smooth bars at various hydrogen concentrations and Péclet numbers ( Pe ). QC failure requires a threshold concentration, initiates at the surface and penetrates deeper as Pe decreases.
