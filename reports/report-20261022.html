01 塑性变形与硬化律
Identifying post-necking hardening of anisotropic materials from a single tensile test orientation across temperatures (2026-10-13)
从单个拉伸试验方向跨温度识别各向异性材料颈缩后硬化
Marta Beltramo, Martina Scapin, Lorenzo Peroni
International Journal of Solids and Structures
对ITER级CuCrZr合金，通过同步双视角记录和360°扫描，识别颈缩后硬化行为并修正等效应力应变响应。
Reliable structural analyses require material models calibrated using experimental data, typically obtained from tensile tests on cylindrical specimens. When anisotropic behavior is involved, the interpretation of such tests becomes significantly more complex, as the specimen cross-sections evolve into non-axisymmetric and generally unknown shapes. The present study proposes a simplified framework for identifying the strain-hardening behavior of anisotropic materials in testing scenarios characterized by limited optical access and availability of only cylindrical specimens with a fixed orientation. The proposed approach employs synchronized recordings from two camera views and a 360° scan of fractured specimens. Under the assumption of elliptical cross-sections, this data is used to reconstruct the evolution of the three-dimensional specimen shape during the test. Based on this reconstruction, the area-based true stress–strain curve is determined and subsequently corrected into an analytical estimate of the equivalent stress–strain response in the post-necking regime. This curve is further refined through a hybrid experimental–numerical procedure involving a single correction iteration and relying on isotropic hardening and simplified assumptions regarding the anisotropic yield behavior and its parameters. Owing to its simplicity, the approach provides a first-order estimate of the strain-hardening law, but it is particularly suited for experimental campaigns in which testing along multiple loading directions is unfeasible. The method offers reduced experimental cost, adaptability to demanding testing environments, and efficient identification. Its applicability is demonstrated through an experimental campaign conducted on an ITER grade CuCrZr alloy and over a wide temperature range up to 580 °C, complementing the currently available hardening data relevant to EU-DEMO divertor applications.
可靠的结构分析需要使用实验数据校准的材料模型，通常通过圆柱试样拉伸试验获得。当涉及各向异性行为时，此类试验的解释变得显著复杂，因为试样横截面会演变为非轴对称且通常未知的形状。本研究提出了一种简化框架，用于在光学访问有限且仅能使用固定方向圆柱试样的测试场景中识别各向异性材料的应变硬化行为。该方法采用同步双视角记录和断裂试样360°扫描，假设横截面为椭圆形，利用这些数据重建测试过程中试样三维形状的演变。基于此重建，确定了基于面积的真实应力应变曲线，并进一步修正为颈缩后等效应力应变响应的分析估计。该曲线通过一种混合实验-数值方法进行优化，涉及单次修正迭代，并依赖于各向同性硬化和对各向异性屈服行为及其参数的简化假设。由于其简便性，该方法提供了应变硬化定律的一阶估计，但特别适用于无法进行多方向测试的实验活动。该方法展示了其在实验活动中的适用性，通过在ITER级CuCrZr合金上进行的广泛温度范围（最高达580°C）测试，补充了目前可用于欧盟-DEMO偏滤器应用的硬化数据。

04 复杂应力状态与屈服准则
Shear-sensitive failure prediction for ultra-thin steel sheets: A combined necking and fracture framework incorporating through-thickness anisotropy (2026-10-21)
超薄钢片剪切敏感失效预测：结合颈缩与断裂的框架考虑厚度方向各向异性
Nan Gu, Shenghao Zhang, Wen Zhang, Xincun Zhuang
International Journal of Damage Mechanics
对0.3 mm BDCK超薄深拉钢，通过结合颈缩与断裂的框架预测剪切相关成形路径下的失效行为。
Failure prediction of ultra-thin deep drawing steel under shear-related forming paths requires the combined assessment of localized necking and ductile fracture. In this study, 0.3 mm BDCK ultra-thin deep drawing steel was characterized by uniaxial tensile and Nakajima tests. The material exhibited weak in-plane anisotropy but pronounced through-thickness anisotropy, with an average r -value of 1.720. A hybrid experimental strategy was used to determine the necking forming limit curve and fracture forming limit curve from near-pure shear to balanced biaxial tension. The results show that localized necking and fracture act as competing failure limits, indicating that both limits should be evaluated within a unified framework. For necking prediction, an updated modified maximum force criterion model accounting for through-thickness anisotropy and shear-sensitive strain-path evolution was employed. For fracture prediction, the Hu-DFC criterion incorporating through-thickness anisotropy was adopted after comparison with DF2012. The framework was applied to a box-shaped deep drawing process. The predicted localized necking and fracture displacements showed relative errors of approximately 8.8% and 7.0%, respectively. The predicted critical region agreed with the experimental fracture location near the die shoulder radius.
超薄深拉钢在剪切相关成形路径下的失效预测需要综合评估局部颈缩和延性断裂。本研究通过单轴拉伸和Nakajima试验对0.3 mm BDCK超薄深拉钢进行了表征。该材料表现出弱的平面内各向异性，但显著的厚度方向各向异性，平均r值为1.720。采用混合实验策略，从近纯剪切到平衡双向拉伸，确定了颈缩成形极限曲线和断裂成形极限曲线。结果表明，局部颈缩和断裂作为竞争失效极限，表明应在统一框架内评估两者。对于颈缩预测，采用了一种考虑厚度方向各向异性和剪切敏感应变路径演化的更新修正最大力准则模型。对于断裂预测，采用Hu-DFC准则，经过与DF2012的比较后，考虑了厚度方向各向异性。该框架应用于盒形深拉工艺。预测的局部颈缩和断裂位移相对误差分别为约8.8%和7.0%。预测的临界区域与实验断裂位置在模具肩部半径附近一致。

05 温度效应与热软化
Characterization of Dynamic Deformation Behavior and Constitutive Model of TB9 Titanium Alloy for Cryogenic-Ultrasonic Elliptical Vibration Cutting (2026-10-19)
基于等温真实应力应变曲线的TB9钛合金动态变形行为与本构模型表征
Ying Meng, Xiuli Fu, Qiaomu Han, Yuanchen Wang, Yongzhi Pan, Chunjun Wang, Chenzhe Zhu
Coatings
对TB9钛合金，通过调整应变率敏感因子和温度指数，开发改进的Johnson-Cook本构模型。
In the cryogenic-ultrasonic elliptical vibration cutting (UEVC) of TB9 titanium alloy, high-strain-rate deformation occurs, and its cryogenic dynamic deformation behavior and constitutive model are crucial for analyzing the cutting process. Static stress–strain curves were obtained using an electronic universal testing machine, while dynamic stress–strain curves under different temperatures and strain rates were measured using a split Hopkinson pressure bar (SHPB) apparatus. Based on the Johnson–Cook (J-C) constitutive model, a modified J-C (M-J-C) constitutive model was developed by adjusting the strain rate sensitivity factor and temperature exponent. The results showed that TB9 exhibited certain strain rate sensitivity and temperature sensitivity. At low temperatures, the flow stress of TB9 titanium alloy first increased and then decreased with strain rate; as the temperature decreased, the flow stress of the material gradually increased. Compared to the original J-C model, the M-J-C curves showed closer descriptive agreement with the reported plastic stress–strain responses within the fitted domain. The proposed model was subsequently implemented in a finite element model of cryogenic-UEVC, and its predictive performance was evaluated through corresponding cutting experiments. Under machining parameter set No. 1, the experimentally measured surface residual-stress magnitude was approximately 355 MPa, whereas the original J-C and M-J-C models predicted 411 MPa and 391 MPa, respectively, reducing the prediction error from 15.7% to 9.8%. For parameter sets Nos. 2–4, the prediction errors of the M-J-C model were lower than those of the original J-C model by 9.4%, 4.2%, and 6.4%, respectively. These results demonstrate that incorporating the coupled effects of strain rate and temperature improves the description of the dynamic deformation behavior of TB9 titanium alloy and enhances the predictive capability of finite element simulations for cryogenic-UEVC.
在低温超声椭圆振动切削（UEVC）中，TB9钛合金会发生高应变率变形，其低温动态变形行为和本构模型对于分析切削过程至关重要。使用电子万能试验机获得静态应力应变曲线，使用分裂霍普金森压力杆（SHPB）装置测量不同温度和应变率下的动态应力应变曲线。基于Johnson-Cook（J-C）本构模型，通过调整应变率敏感因子和温度指数，开发了改进的J-C（M-J-C）本构模型。结果表明，TB9钛合金表现出一定的应变率敏感性和温度敏感性。在低温下，TB9钛合金的流动应力随应变率先增加后减少；随着温度降低，材料的流动应力逐渐增加。与原始J-C模型相比，M-J-C曲线在拟合范围内更接近已报道的塑性应力应变响应。该模型随后被应用于低温UEVC的有限元模型中，并通过相应的切削实验评估其预测性能。在加工参数集No. 1下，实验测得的表面残余应力幅值约为355 MPa，而原始J-C和M-J-C模型分别预测为411 MPa和391 MPa，预测误差分别从15.7%降低至9.8%。对于参数集No. 2–4，M-J-C模型的预测误差分别比原始J-C模型降低了9.4%、4.2%和6.4%。这些结果表明，结合应变率和温度的耦合效应可以改善TB9钛合金动态变形行为的描述，并增强低温UEVC有限元模拟的预测能力。

09 用户子程序与数值实现
Abaqus implementation of the phase-field approach to fracture (2026-10-17)
断裂相场方法在Abaqus中的实现
Jiangwei Zhou, Victor Lefèvre, Oscar Lopez‐Pamies
arXiv (Cornell University)
对脆性材料采用相场模型，通过UMAT子程序实现，得到裂纹演化结果。
In this paper, we introduce an Abaqus user-subroutine implementation of the phase-field model of fracture initiated by Kumar, Francfort, and Lopez-Pamies (\emph{J. Mech. Phys. Solids} 112, 523--551, 2018). The implementation is presented for the basic case of isotropic linear elastic brittle materials with a Drucker-Prager strength surface. The material inputs are therefore the Young's modulus $E$, the Poisson's ratio $ν$, the uniaxial tensile strength $\sts$, the uniaxial compressive strength $\scs$, and the fracture toughness $G_c$. The mathematical correspondence between the heat equation and the evolution equation for the phase field $v(\bfX,t)$ is exploited through a UMAT subroutine, wherein the driving force $c_{\texttt{e}}(\bfX,t)$ --- accounting for the material strength surface --- in the phase-field model plays the role of a heat source. To ensure that the equations for the displacement field $\bfu(\bfX,t)$ and the phase field $v(\bfX,t)$ are solved in a staggered manner --- a solution strategy that, as opposed to monolithic approaches, reliably yields the correct physical solution --- we make use of a combination of UEXTERNALDB, UAMP, MPC, and URDFIL subroutines. Within this approach, the MPC and URDFIL subroutines are additionally utilized to enforce the required bounding and irreversibility inequalities on $v(\bfX,t)$ via a primal-dual active set approach. ABSTRACT This study experimentally and numerically investigates fatigue damage in additively manufactured 316L stainless steel produced by selective laser melting using the powder bed fusion–laser beam process. More than 100 specimens were tested under tensile and strain‐controlled fatigue loading to evaluate the effects of surface condition, hot isostatic pressing (HIP), and build orientation. Machining provides the greatest improvement in fatigue performance by removing sub‐surface defects, while HIP provides additional benefits through reduced porosity. Vertically built specimens exhibit approximately 40% longer fatigue life than diagonal and horizontal specimens because elongated grains aligned with the build direction enhance ductility and stabilize crack propagation. A unified continuum damage mechanics‐based constitutive model predicts cyclic softening and fatigue life across low‐ and high‐cycle regimes. Fatigue anisotropy is represented by an orientation‐dependent fatigue ductility coefficient, while remaining parameters are orientation‐independent. The model accurately reproduces cyclic hysteresis loops and fatigue life to failure.
