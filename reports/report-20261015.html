Impact of coupling a constitutive model with the ductile fracture criterion on the computational predictability of wrought Inconel 718 (2026-10-12)
将本构模型与延性断裂准则耦合对锻造Inconel 718计算预测性的影响
Petr Kubík, Josef Zapletal, Tomáš Návrat, František Šebek
International Journal of Damage Mechanics
对锻造Inconel 718材料进行拉伸试验，比较不同模型的力响应和断裂特征，发现非局部方法在处理大空间问题上尚不成熟。
Prediction of ductile fracture is essential for designing of components and structures. Local uncoupled and local as well as non-local partially coupled models are compared for tensile tests of specimens made from nickel-based superalloy Inconel 718 with varying geometries: a smooth cylindrical specimen, notched cylindrical specimens with R2.5 and R8 and a flat plate specimen. The ductile fracture criterion was calibrated using a hybrid experimental–numerical approach, with the non-local part based on strain in the loading direction measured optically on a notched specimen. Models are compared with respect to force responses, moment of crack initiation and appearance of final failure. All numerical simulations, including a mesh sensitivity study, were performed in LS-DYNA software using the Newmark implicit time integration scheme. Globally, the non-local approach does not seem to be ready for solving bulky spatial problems.
预测延性断裂对于组件和结构设计至关重要。对不同几何形状的镍基高温合金Inconel 718试样进行拉伸试验，比较了局部非耦合和局部及非局部部分耦合模型。延性断裂准则通过混合实验-数值方法进行标定，其中非局部部分基于未缺口试样在加载方向上的应变光学测量。模型通过力响应、裂纹起始矩和最终失效出现情况进行比较。所有数值模拟，包括网格敏感性研究，均在LS-DYNA软件中使用Newmark隐式时间积分方案进行。总体来看，非局部方法似乎尚未准备好解决大空间问题。

Triaxiality-dependent asymmetric plasticity model covering a wide range of stress states for magnesium alloy sheets: Modeling shear and near-plane strain anisotropy (2026-10-08)
基于应力三轴度的镁合金板带宽应力状态下的非对称塑性模型：建模剪切和近平面应变各向异性
Yanqiang Ren, Kai Du, Wentao Zheng, Liang Zhang, Li Dong, Haizheng Yu, Yong Ping Hou, Hongjun Huang
Journal of Magnesium and Alloys
提出一种基于多项式函数和应力三轴度耦合的新型各向异性非对称屈服准则，用于准确预测镁合金板的塑性行为。
The pronounced tension-compression asymmetry and anisotropy are common problems that severely restrict the forming accuracy of magnesium alloy components. An effective solution strategy involves adopting high-precision yield criteria capable of reconstructing material properties to optimize process parameters. In this work, a novel anisotropic-asymmetric yield criterion based on a coupling strategy of polynomial functions and stress triaxiality is proposed. The model delivers high flexibility and accuracy in capturing the anisotropic and strongly asymmetric response of magnesium alloy sheets, as validated on eight widely different virtual material cases. A systematic calibration procedure for determining the material parameters in both the yield stress function and the plastic potential function of the new model is also provided. Finally, the proposed model was applied to the AZ31 and AZ31B magnesium alloy sheets with markedly different yield loci. Compared with the CPB06ex2, Hu2017, and Zhou2023 asymmetric yield criteria, the new model achieves accurate predictions for 15 stress-related sampling points and 12 strain-related sampling points across six stress states, demonstrating comprehensively superior prediction accuracy. More importantly, the new model accurately captures the anisotropic response characteristics of the AZ31 and AZ31B magnesium alloy sheets under shear and near-plane strain stress states, strongly confirming its accuracy and applicability.
显著的张拉-压缩不对称性和各向异性是限制镁合金组件成形精度的常见问题。有效的解决策略是采用高精度屈服准则，以重建材料特性并优化工艺参数。本文提出了一种基于多项式函数与应力三轴度耦合的新各向异性非对称屈服准则。该模型在捕捉镁合金板的各向异性与强不对称响应方面表现出高灵活性和准确性，已在八个差异显著的虚拟材料案例中验证。还提供了一种系统校准程序，用于确定新模型屈服应力函数和塑性势函数中的材料参数。最后，该模型应用于具有明显不同屈服轨迹的AZ31和AZ31B镁合金板。与CPB06ex2、Hu2017和Zhou2023等非对称屈服准则相比，新模型在六个应力状态下对15个应力相关采样点和12个应变相关采样点实现了准确预测，显示出全面优越的预测精度。更重要的是，该模型准确捕捉了AZ31和AZ31B镁合金板在剪切和近平面应变应力状态下的各向异性响应特性，强烈证实了其准确性和适用性。

Dynamic Compressive Behavior and Deformation-Failure Characteristics of a Medium-Carbon Cr-Ni-Mo High-Strength Steel (2026-10-13)
中碳Cr-Ni-Mo高强度钢的动态压缩行为与变形-失效特性
Yi Qu, Ao Zhang, Ronghua Su, Bo Yang, Zhenlong Chao, Yunhou Sun
Materials
通过准静态拉伸和压缩试验及SHPB动态压缩试验，研究中碳Cr-Ni-Mo高强度钢在不同应变率和温度下的动态压缩行为与变形失效特性。
To investigate the dynamic compressive behavior and deformation characteristics of medium-carbon Cr-Ni-Mo high-strength steel under different strain rates and temperatures, quasi-static tensile and compression tests, together with Split Hopkinson Pressure Bar (SHPB) dynamic compression tests, were conducted. The Johnson–Cook flow stress and damage model parameters were calibrated based on the experimental results under the investigated loading conditions. Combined with macroscopic morphologies of recovered specimens, SEM fracture observations, and finite element simulations, the deformation and damage behavior of the material was investigated. The results show that the material exhibits pronounced strain-rate strengthening and thermal softening characteristics. At 298 K, increasing the strain rate from 1000 to 5000 s−1 increases the mean maximum compressive stress from 2263.1 to 2500.9 MPa, while expanding the plastic deformation range. At 1000 s−1, increasing the temperature from 298 to 698 K decreases the mean maximum compressive stress from 2263.1 to 1722.2 MPa. The calibrated Johnson–Cook model reasonably describes the stress response and deformation characteristics at different strain rates at 298 K, with relative errors of simulated maximum compressive stresses below 4% for the representative experimental curves used in the numerical comparison. The macroscopic and microscopic observations reveal that increasing strain rate promotes the localized shear deformation, inclined cracking, and partial fragmentation after plastic compression, with enhanced shear deformation features reflected by elongated dimples, fracture steps, and tear ridges. Increasing temperature reduces fragmentation under high strain-rate loading and promotes the formation of coarse dimples and plastic flow features. Numerical analysis further indicates that increasing strain rate promotes the localization of equivalent plastic strain, leading to inclined shear bands and crack formation.
为研究中碳Cr-Ni-Mo高强度钢在不同应变率和温度下的动态压缩行为及变形失效特性，进行了准静态拉伸和压缩试验，以及Split Hopkinson Pressure Bar (SHPB) 动态压缩试验。Johnson–Cook 流变应力和损伤模型参数基于所研究的加载条件下的实验结果进行标定。结合恢复试样的宏观形貌、扫描电镜断口观察和有限元模拟，研究了材料的变形和损伤行为。结果表明，该材料表现出显著的应变率强化和热软化特性。在298 K时，应变率从1000增加到5000 s−1，平均最大压缩应力从2263.1增加到2500.9 MPa，同时扩展了塑性变形范围。在1000 s−1时，温度从298 K增加到698 K，平均最大压缩应力从2263.1降低到1722.2 MPa。标定的Johnson–Cook模型合理描述了298 K下不同应变率下的应力响应和变形特性，数值对比中所用代表性实验曲线的模拟最大压缩应力相对误差低于4%。宏观和微观观察表明，应变率增加促进了局部剪切变形、倾斜裂纹和塑性压缩后的部分碎裂，剪切变形特征增强表现为拉长的缩孔、断裂台阶和撕裂脊。温度升高则在高应变率加载下减少碎裂，促进粗缩孔和塑性流动特征的形成。数值分析进一步表明，应变率增加促进了等效塑性应变的局部化，导致倾斜剪切带和裂纹形成。

Genetic algorithm–based calibration of the Johnson–Cook fracture model for high strain rate conditions (2026-09-30)
基于遗传算法的Johnson-Cook断裂模型在高应变率条件下的校准
Yun-Sang Lee, Gyeong-Han Lee, Jaehyeong Lim, Sang‐Woo Kim, Sooyong Lee
International Journal of Impact Engineering
已确认实体匹配：International Journal of Impact Engineering（期刊/会议）
对6061-T6铝合金采用遗传算法校准Johnson-Cook断裂模型参数，通过Taylor冲击试验结果，得到参数D1-D4并验证其在高速冲击下的预测精度。
Recent studies have increasingly employed optimization algorithms, including genetic algorithms, to identify Johnson–Cook material model parameters while reducing the experimental burden required for conventional parameter identification. However, most of these studies are based on material behavior obtained from low-strain rate tests, such as quasi-static tensile tests and notched specimen tests and therefore may not fully capture the coupled effects of strain rate dependence and damage evolution occurring during high-velocity impact. To address this limitation, the present study performed genetic algorithm-based optimization using Taylor impact test results and calibrated the Johnson–Cook fracture model parameters of aluminum 6061-T6 while keeping D 5 fixed at the literature-reported value. The derived parameters were D 1 = −0.583, D 2 = 1.451, D 3 = −0.378, and D 4 = 0.002. The calibrated parameter set was further assessed through high-velocity impact simulations of 6 mm and 8 mm thick aluminum 6061-T6 plates under a different impact configuration using the same literature-derived material data. The predicted penetration speeds and diameters showed good agreement with experimental measurements, with maximum deviations of 7.18% in penetration speed and 2.94% in penetration diameter for 6 mm and 8 mm thick plates under high-velocity impact conditions. Beyond final-state agreement, the energy erosion responses were compared using target-plate internal energy, eroded volume fraction, and eroded internal energy. These quantities were used as supplementary numerical indicators to assess element-deletion-related energy loss within the adopted finite element framework. The repeatability of the identified parameter set with respect to stochastic variability in the GA remains to be further assessed through repeated optimizations using different random seeds.
近期研究越来越多地采用优化算法，包括遗传算法，以识别Johnson-Cook材料模型参数，同时减少传统参数识别所需的实验负担。然而，大多数研究基于低应变率测试获得的材料行为，如准静态拉伸试验和带缺口试样试验，因此可能无法充分捕捉高速冲击过程中应变率依赖性和损伤演化之间的耦合效应。为解决这一局限性，本研究使用Taylor冲击试验结果进行基于遗传算法的优化，并在校准6061-T6铝合金的Johnson-Cook断裂模型参数时保持D5固定在文献报告值。所得到的参数为D1 = −0.583，D2 = 1.451，D3 = −0.378，D4 = 0.002。通过使用相同的文献数据集，进一步评估了校准参数集在不同冲击配置下6 mm和8 mm厚6061-T6铝合金板的高速冲击模拟结果。预测的穿透速度和直径与实验测量值表现出良好一致性，最大偏差分别为6 mm和8 mm厚板的穿透速度7.18%和穿透直径2.94%。除了最终状态的一致性，还通过目标板内部能量、侵蚀体积分数和侵蚀内部能量比较了能量侵蚀响应。这些量被用作补充的数值指标，以评估所采用有限元框架中与元素删除相关的能量损失。所识别参数集的可重复性仍需通过使用不同随机种子进行重复优化进一步评估。

Dynamic mechanical properties of near-alpha TA19 titanium alloy and application of calibrated constitutive models in cutting simulation (2026-10-09)
近α型TA19钛合金的动态力学性能及校准本构模型在切削模拟中的应用
Limei Liu, Dongliang Wang, Jilin Zhang, Hai-Shen Jia, Xiangbin Yi, Baodong Li
Materials Research Express
通过准静态压缩和Split Hopkinson压力杆试验研究TA19钛合金的动态力学性能，校准PL和JC本构模型并评估其在切削模拟中的适用性。
Abstract Accurate characterization of flow-stress behavior is essential for modeling the dynamic deformation and machining response of titanium alloys. In this study, the mechanical behavior of near-alpha TA19 titanium alloy was investigated by quasi-static compression tests at strain rates of 0.001-0.01 s-1 and split Hopkinson pressure bar tests at 25-650 °C and 1500-4500 s-1. Strain-rate sensitivity, thermal softening, and adiabatic temperature rise were analyzed, and measured flow stresses were corrected to obtain isothermal stress-strain curves. Power-Law (PL) and Johnson-Cook (JC) constitutive models were calibrated using the same experimental dataset and quantitatively evaluated. TA19 exhibited positive strain-rate sensitivity and pronounced thermal softening. As the strain rate increased from 1500 to 4500 s-1, the average flow stress increased by 112.447-228.009 MPa, depending on temperature, whereas increasing the temperature from 25 to 650 °C reduced it by 205.226-294.361 MPa. Adiabatic heating increased with strain rate at room temperature but was generally suppressed at elevated initial temperatures. Under 16 dynamic conditions, the PL and JC models produced average absolute relative error ranges of 1.20%-7.24% and 1.54%-12.38%, respectively, with overall values of 3.85% and 6.36%. The PL model therefore provided higher overall prediction accuracy, whereas the JC model offered better numerical compatibility for engineering simulation. The calibrated JC model was implemented in AdvantEdge to investigate the effects of feed rate, cutting speed, and rake angle. Increasing feed rate reduced shear strain, plastic strain rate, and normalized cutting loads. Increasing cutting speed markedly increased plastic strain rate and redistributed the load between the cutting- and thrust-force components.
