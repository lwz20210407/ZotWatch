The Strength–Ductility Frontier: Just-in-Time Hardening, the Limits of Uniform Deformation, and a Design Rule for Strong, Ductile Alloys (2026-11-23)
强度-延性前沿：即时硬化、均匀变形的极限及强延性合金的设计规则
Fitih M. Cinnor
Zenodo (CERN European Organization for Nuclear Research)
研究金属在颈缩前的硬化储备，提出强度-延性设计规则，确定不同硬化条件下的临界应变值。
A metal can begin to neck with substantial hardening still in reserve. We separate the size of that reserve from the schedule that delivers it. For homogeneous, rate-independent tension with reserve equal to yield stress, the sharp limits for positive exponential relaxation, non-increasing hardening and the marginal ideal are 0.232, 0.500 and 0.693 true plastic strain. A single rate is optimal over the entire relaxing class, yet even that rate needs more than e times the marginal reserve at equal nonzero strain below one. The penalty belongs to exponential relaxation, not to a falling tangent alone. The inverse test gives two design quantities: the stress the mechanism must add and the hardening rate it must still supply at the target strain. Measured tensile endpoints can rule out a non-increasing tangent; three early stress readings can bound the first instability for positive, non-increasing, log-convex hardening. In 90 specimens from 22 series, 90 nominal lower bounds and 68 lower bounds tested throughout a ±0.5 MPa stress box lie below the sampled peak proxy. The prespecified SUS301L 5%-martensite onset window missed the observed optimum, separating that phase-marker rule from the mechanical timing law. A survey of 2,174 alloy-condition entries across three compilations yields an atlas of 805 scored tensile records. Among 300 records at or above 500 MPa yield, 2 or 15 cross a Voce-inferred headroom of 3% of shear modulus, depending on the elongation convention; the best-passive minimum gives 0 or 11 crossings. In the 796-record endpoint comparison, the inferred pre-peak stress rise is approximately flat against yield but declines relative to yield. A full-curve check found that the proxy overestimated scheduling efficiency by 31% in the two sheet alloys tested. The atlas ranks inferred requirements and flags cases for direct testing. The design studies turn the bounds into three decisions. For a conductive copper contact, we specify the particle hardening needed for 680 MPa proof stress and 12% uniform elongation at a same-state target of 75% IACS. In the separate rigid-plastic comparison at 680 MPa plastic onset, the same elongation calls for 258 MPa of relaxing reserve versus 81.6 MPa at marginal stability. For laminated-titanium, we predict where delaying transformation can cause an abrupt loss of uniform strain. For β cryogenic steel, a cylinder calculation shows that the grade order can reverse at unchanged phase-marker strains. Each result identifies what hardening the design needs and the measurement that would decide whether the material can supply it. 02 塑性变形与硬化律
Unified post-necking true stress-strain models for hot-rolled steels (2026-11-18)
用于热轧钢的统一颈缩后真实应力-应变模型
Jingsheng Zhou, Leroy Gardner
Structures
开发三种标准化颈缩后真实应力-应变模型，用于任意应力状态下的材料行为预测。
The use of advanced numerical methods for the simulation and design of steel components and structures has increased rapidly in recent years. The response of elements experiencing large plastic deformations and even fracture can now be accurately captured. For such analyses, the full true stress-strain relationship of the material up to the fracture strain is required. While pre-necking stress-strain behaviour has been extensively studied, the post-necking behaviour remains less well defined and there are no standardised predictive models. This paper focuses on the post-necking true stress-strain behaviour of hot-rolled steels. Three alternative standardised true stress-strain models for use under any stress state are developed – a Nonlinear post-necking Model (NM), a Simplified Nonlinear post-necking Model (SNM) and a One-stage Nonlinear hardening Model (ONM). Empirical predictive equations for the key post-necking material properties, including the reference true fracture stress and strain, and the roundness of the post-necking true stress-strain curve are developed. The developed predictive equations were derived from a dataset comprising 256 standard tensile coupon tests on hot-rolled steels. Since post-necking behaviour cannot be obtained directly from experimental measurements, each coupon was first numerically simulated, and the key material parameters were extracted from the numerical results. The accuracy of the proposed equations was evaluated and shown to provide good predictive performance, demonstrating their applicability in facilitating the increasing use of advanced computational methods for the analysis and design of steel structures. The SNM formulation, together with the associated predictive equations, provides both high prediction accuracy and a simple functional form, while satisfying the Considère criterion and remaining compatible with existing pre-necking models. The SNM formulation has been accepted for inclusion in EN 1993–1–14, and is suitable, in conjunction with appropriate strain limits and partial factors, for use in both numerical design calculations and simulations.

03 复杂应力状态与屈服准则
Nonlocal plasticity framework for stress-state-dependent fracture limit diagram in sheet metal (2026-11-20)
用于薄板金属应力状态依赖断裂极限图的非局部塑性框架
Zhichao Wei, Guoxi Mao, Shuwei Zhou, Emad Scharifi, Sebastian Münstermann
Mechanics of Materials
提出非局部非关联塑性框架，用于预测EN AW 6082铝合金薄板的应力状态依赖断裂成形极限。
This paper presents a nonlocal non-associated plasticity framework for predicting plastic deformation and characterizing stress-state-dependent fracture forming limits of EN AW 6082 aluminum alloy sheets. A combined hardening law, together with the Drucker–Prager yield condition and a non-associated von Mises flow rule, is used to describe plastic behavior under large deformations. A plastic predictor–elastic corrector method is employed for the numerical implementation of the proposed nonlocal plasticity model. The main contribution of this work is the numerical implementation of an integral-based nonlocal plasticity formulation within the Abaqus finite-element framework, enabling the regularization of localized plastic deformation and stress-state variables relevant to ductile fracture. An explicit UEXTERNALDB-based approach is implemented in Abaqus together with a user-defined subroutine. The formulation enables the equivalent plastic strain and stress-state variables, including stress triaxiality and the Lode parameter, to be calculated nonlocally within a conventional finite-element framework, thereby reducing mesh dependency associated with strain localization. Nakajima tests with different specimen geometries are conducted to investigate the formability of thin aluminum alloy sheets. A modified stress-state-dependent, strain-based Bai–Wierzbicki (BW) ductile fracture model is employed to characterize fracture behavior. In contrast to the conventional forming-limit-curve approach, the fracture forming limit diagram is derived from the stress-state-dependent ductile fracture model. Digital image correlation is used to measure the strain fields during the Nakajima tests. The results demonstrate that the nonlocal plasticity formulation reduces mesh sensitivity associated with localized deformation and stress-state characterization, while the modified BW fracture criterion improves the description of stress-state-dependent fracture forming limits. By combining nonlocal regularization with an enhanced stress-state-dependent fracture criterion, the proposed framework provides a robust numerical basis for the analysis of fracture forming limits in sheet metals.

Stress-State-Dependent Adiabatic Shear Band Initiation in Martensitic Steel under Dynamic Loading (2026-11-01)

Understanding the stress-state dependence of adiabatic shear band (ASB) initiation is essential for the modeling and design of high-strength steels used in extreme dynamic applications. In this study, the high strain-rate and failure behavior of Armox 500T are systematically investigated under proportional axisymmetric tension, compression, and compression-shear loading. Direct compression and split-tensile Hopkinson bar experiments are combined with inclined compression-shear specimens to isolate Lode-angle-dependent effects on strain localization, ductility, and ASB initiation. Full-field digital image correlation (DIC), together with optical, scanning, and transmission electron microscopy is used to correlate macroscopic mechanical response with microstructural evolution. The results reveal pronounced asymmetry in high strain-rate behavior. Dynamic tension exhibits enhanced strengthening (up to 12%) and sustained ductility, whereas dynamic compression loading results in over 50% embrittlement due to ASB formation. Furthermore, enhanced shear loading increases the propensity to ASB initation leading to severe embrittlement driven by intense shear localization along the plane of maximum shear. Microstructural analysis reveals that ASB initiation is an intrinsic material response and energy-absorbing mechanisms remain active following band formation. By correlating interrupted dynamic tests with in-situ strain measurements, an experimentally defined, microstructurally validated instability strain associated with stress-state dependent ASB initiation is identified. It is revealed that under dynamic loading, the equivalent instability and fracture strains decrease by over 50% with increasing shear loading from uniaxial compression.

Stress-State-Dependent Ductile Fracture Modeling and Burst Pressure Prediction for Alloy 690TT Steam Generator Tubes with Axial Through-Wall Cracks (2026-11-20)
合金690TT蒸汽发生器管轴向贯穿裂纹的应力状态依赖延性断裂建模与爆破压力预测
Bin Zhang, Naibin Jiang
Materials
基于延性断裂模型，预测合金690TT蒸汽发生器管轴向贯穿裂纹的爆破压力，与实验数据吻合良好。
Steam generator tubes in pressurized water reactors may develop defects during long-term service, potentially leading to rupture. This study develops a ductile-fracture-based finite element framework for predicting the burst pressure of Alloy 690TT steam generator tubes containing axial through-wall cracks. The material response is described using the Johnson–Cook plasticity model coupled with a ductile damage model that accounts for stress triaxiality and the Lode parameter. Material parameters were calibrated using tensile test data obtained under different loading conditions and stress states. As the strain-rate effect on fracture strain was limited within the investigated range, strain-rate dependence was incorporated into the plasticity model but neglected in the damage model. The calibrated model was validated against tensile test results and subsequently applied to tubes containing axial through-wall cracks of different lengths. Mechanism-Aware Versus Mechanism-Agnostic Modeling of Void-Mediated Failure in Nominally Isotropic Magnesium Alloys (2026-03-07)

Neha Arora, Shailendra P. Joshi
Journal of Applied Mechanics
对名义各向同性镁合金的空洞介导失效进行机制感知与机制无关建模对比分析。
Weakly-textured magnesium (Mg) alloys often exhibit nominally isotropic stress–strain behavior with muted tension–compression asymmetry, making an isotropic plasticity assumption appear reasonable. Here, we assess the validity of that assumption for ductile damage governed by void growth and coalescence. The response of a nominally isotropic Mg alloy is represented using three constitutive descriptions: isotropic J2 plasticity, orthotropic HILL plasticity, and a mechanism-based multi-surface plasticity formulation. Finite-deformation finite-element simulations are performed on a porous periodic unit cell with fully periodic boundary conditions, subjected to constant macroscopic stress states corresponding to purely tensile loading and combined tension–shear loading. The results show that the damage response depends sensitively on the evolving distribution of deformation mechanisms around the void, which cannot be reproduced by isotropic matrix idealization. Importantly, the analysis suggests a subtle yet important role of these mechanisms, which extends beyond setting the net plastic anisotropy that can affect the predicted ductility.
弱织构的镁（Mg）合金通常表现出名义各向同性的应力-应变行为，且拉压不对称性较弱，使得采用各向同性塑性假设看起来合理。本文评估了该假设在延性损伤由空洞生长和合并主导情况下的有效性。通过三种本构描述来表示名义各向同性镁合金的响应：各向同性J2塑性、正交各向异性HILL塑性以及基于机制的多面塑性公式。在恒定宏观应力状态下，对具有完全周期边界条件的多孔周期性单元进行有限变形有限元模拟，该应力状态对应纯拉伸载荷和组合拉伸-剪切载荷。结果表明，损伤响应对空洞周围变形机制的演变分布非常敏感，而各向同性基体理想化无法再现这种分布。重要的是，分析表明这些机制在失效过程中起着微妙但重要的作用，其影响不仅限于设定净塑性各向异性，还可能影响预测的延性。
