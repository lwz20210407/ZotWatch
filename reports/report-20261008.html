Transferability of classical uncoupled ductile-damage criteria to fracture prediction in single-point incremental forming over a controlled geometrical process window (2026-09-30)
经典非耦合延性损伤准则在受控几何工艺窗口下单点增量成形中断裂预测的可迁移性
José-David Caicedo Bohorquez, Idriss Tiba, Yessine Ayed, Sandra Chevret-Zimmer, Tudor Balan
Journal of Materials Processing Technology
对单点增量成形中非比例和非单调加载的材料，评估经典非耦合延性损伤准则在断裂预测中的可迁移性，发现其无法准确反映实验顺序。
Uncoupled ductile damage criteria applied to processes involving non-proportional and non-monotonic loading, such as single-point incremental forming (SPIF), are commonly calibrated using standard characterization tests conducted under proportional monotonic loading. Prediction discrepancies may reflect incomplete coverage of the calibration domain or limitations of the criterion formulation. This study examines both explanations, evaluating transferability at three levels: discrimination between fractured and non-fractured configurations, preservation of their relative experimental ordering, and fracture localization. A process window for AA5754-H111, defined by wall angle and in-plane shape, comprised nine configurations spanning the transition from non-fractured to consistently fractured conditions. The Modified Mohr–Coulomb, Cockcroft–Latham, and Johnson–Cook criteria were assessed by comparing the experimental fracture results with predictions obtained from finite-element simulations validated against surface strain fields measured by digital image correlation. All three criteria predicted fracture-threshold crossing in every configuration, including non-fractured ones, and did not preserve the complete experimental ordering. Their damage maxima nevertheless lay within the fractured region in two of the three consistently fractured cases and approximately 15 mm from the crack in the third, compared with 30 mm for the equivalent-plastic-strain maximum. Nonlinear accumulation restored neither discrimination nor ordering, whereas mapping the calibrated loci as fracture forming limit curves better distinguished fractured and non-fractured configurations in principal-strain space. A joint calibration of the Modified Mohr–Coulomb criterion combining the characterization tests with fractured SPIF cases still produced mean absolute percentage errors in equivalent plastic strain at fracture of 46.1% for the characterization tests and 31.0% for the SPIF cases, indicating that adding process data did not resolve the conflict within this functional form. The conflict therefore cannot be attributed to calibration coverage alone. Process data can establish whether a common calibration exists, but their inclusion alone cannot reconcile the criterion formulation with both characterization and process histories.

Abstract Dual-phase Mg–Li alloys display interesting mechanical properties, including an unusual increase in ductility at low strain rates due to grain refinement. The effects of grain size, testing temperature and strain rate on the flow stress of an Mg-8wt.% Li alloy processed by high-pressure torsion were investigated through mechanical tests at low strain rates and through machine learning algorithms trained on data in the literature. The data are also compared to the deformation mechanism of grain boundary sliding. The experimental results and creep analyses show a high strain rate sensitivity and ductility and a low activation energy in the material, suggesting the dominance of thermal activated deformation mechanisms. The mechanism of grain boundary sliding is able to predict the trends revealed by experiments, while machine learning models suggest the temperature as the most significant parameter in flow stress. The ability of the black-box and white-box machine learning algorithms to make predictions and errors of models is discussed.

In-situ resistance heating-ultrasonic vibration composite forming of TC4 alloy sheet: Experimental and numerical study (2026-10-01)
TC4合金板在原位电阻加热-超声振动复合成形中的研究：实验与数值分析
高猛, Mengtao Ning, Dong-Xu Wen, Liang Huang, Li Jianjun
Journal of Materials Processing Technology
对TC4合金板进行原位电阻加热-超声振动复合成形实验与数值分析，揭示其微结构调控机制及成形性能提升。
To improve the manufacturing efficiency and dimensional accuracy of aerospace TC4 alloy sheet components, an in-situ resistance heating and ultrasonic vibration-assisted V-bending (RHUAV) composite forming process was developed. Increasing vibration amplitude enhances acoustic softening and reduces forming force and springback, whereas increasing ultrasonic frequency weakens these effects because of ultrasonic energy attenuation. More importantly, a thermo-ultrasonic microstructure-regulation mechanism is revealed, linking phase transformation, grain rotation, and dislocation redistribution to improved deformation coordination. This coupled regulation promotes dynamic recrystallization and grain refinement during RHUAV forming. Based on these mechanisms, a dislocation-density-based unified constitutive model and a process parameter–forming effect mapping model were established to predict dislocation evolution, flow behavior, forming force, and springback, with prediction errors below 4%. Engineering validation was performed using the base plate of an aero-engine flow path plate. Compared with conventional isothermal forming, RHUAV reduced the short- and long-side springback ratios by 63.3% and 77.8%, increased impact toughness by 19.3%, and improved manufacturing efficiency by 80.2%, while also refining the microstructure and improving surface quality. These results demonstrate that RHUAV provides an efficient route for integrated shape–property control of aerospace titanium alloy sheet components.

08 复杂应力状态与屈服准则
Role of post-necking softening in the ductile failure of pure titanium (2026-10-03)
纯钛延性断裂中颈缩后软化的作用
Mehdi Ganjiani, Javad Ghafari, František Šebek
Materials Today Communications
针对纯钛材料，研究颈缩后软化对延性断裂的影响，建立改进的塑性-损伤耦合模型以准确预测其力学行为。
Commercially pure titanium grade 2 exhibits a distinct deformation profile: a short strain hardening stage followed by prolonged post-necking ductility, behavior inadequately captured by conventional hardening laws. To address this, a modified coupled plasticity–damage model is proposed to accurately reproduce the stress–strain response of the material. The study systematically investigates ductile failure under variable stress triaxialities and Lode angles using five distinct specimen geometries: dog-bone, notched, two in-plane shear configurations, and hat-shaped compression. A fully coupled ductile damage–plasticity model, explicitly accounting for stress triaxiality and Lode angle, is implemented alongside a non-linear damage evolution law. Three modified Ganjiani, KHPS, and DF2016 failure criteria are studied due to their comparable predictive accuracy. Experimental data are validated through ABAQUS/Explicit simulations using VUSDFLD and VUHARD, demonstrating good agreement in force evolution and fracture strain across all geometries. Digital image correlation further confirms the fidelity of the simulations. The initiation of failure is geometry dependent: for dog-bone and notched specimens, it coincides with the onset of slope reduction post-plateau; for shear, shear-tension, and hat-shaped compression specimens, failure initiates at the onset of hardening, where the slope of the force–displacement curve begins to decrease significantly. These findings provide a robust framework for predicting ductile failure in the material studied under complex loading.

Arbitrary-order virtual element methods for high-order phase-field modeling of ductile fracture (2026-10-02)
用于高阶相场模拟的任意阶虚拟元素方法
Yu Leng, Quoc-Thai Tran, Ricardo A. Lebensohn, Gianmarco Manzini, Hashem M. Mourad
Computer Methods in Applied Mechanics and Engineering
针对金属材料的延性断裂，采用任意阶虚拟元素方法结合四阶相场模型与速率相关塑性，实现复杂断裂现象的模拟。
Ductile fracture in metallic materials involves substantial plastic deformation, shear localization, damage accumulation, and eventual crack propagation—phenomena that pose significant computational challenges when combined. We present an arbitrary-order virtual element method (VEM) for ductile fracture using a fourth-order phase-field formulation coupled with rate-dependent plasticity. The fourth-order phase-field regularization provides sharper crack representation and improved accuracy compared to standard second-order models, but requires 𝐶 1 -continuous elements that complicate numerical implementation. We address this challenge by employing 𝐻 2 -conforming VEM for the fourth-order phase-field equation and 𝐻 1 -conforming VEM for the momentum balance equation. The plastic response is modeled using the Bodner–Partom viscoplastic constitutive model with a plastic degradation function that couples damage evolution to plastic deformation. Numerical examples demonstrate the capability to simulate complex ductile fracture phenomena including shear band formation, crack nucleation from plastically deformed regions, and dynamic crack propagation. The results demonstrate this framework as a powerful computational tool for modeling ductile fracture processes using polygonal elements.

A symbolic regression-corrected Arrhenius constitutive model for high-temperature deformation based on deep symbolic optimization (2026-09-22)
基于深度符号优化的修正Arrhenius本构模型用于高温变形
Xiaoge Quan, Xuechao Liu, Yongfeng Chang, Peng Han, Jun Cai, Wulin Sun, Hongsheng Man
Journal of Materials Research and Technology
针对高温变形，基于深度符号优化开发修正Arrhenius本构模型，提升对材料流动应力的预测精度。
During high-temperature plastic deformation, material flow stress is jointly affected by deformation temperature, strain rate, and strain. Its accurate prediction is essential for hot-working process optimization and finite element simulation. To address the limitations associated with the predefined functional form and residual prediction errors of the strain-compensated Arrhenius constitutive model, a symbolic-regression-corrected strain-compensated Arrhenius model based on deep symbolic optimization, termed SR-ARH, was developed using hot-compression data for ECO-AlMag8, Haynes 230, Cu-Sn alloy, and TC4. The framework employs an RNN-guided, GP-assisted strategy to identify explicit correction-factor expressions. The operator set was constrained according to the nonperiodic and nonlinear characteristics of high-temperature flow-stress evolution. Based on feature-importance analysis, SR-ARH-2F was developed using temperature and strain rate, followed by SR-ARH-3F with strain as an additional feature. The SR-ARH-3F model exhibited superior overall predictive performance compared with the SR-ARH-2F model and was therefore selected as the final model. Leave-one-condition-out validation revealed condition-dependent generalization performance under unseen deformation conditions, while strain-hardening-rate analysis showed no obvious nonphysical oscillations within the calibrated range. Compared with the strain-compensated Arrhenius model, the SR-ARH-3F model reduced RMSE by 3.397-15.574 MPa and AARE by 3.428-8.177 percentage points, while increasing R by 0.00616-0.04747.
